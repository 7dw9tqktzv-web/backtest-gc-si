--- OPTIMIZATIONS APPLIED ---

Step 1: Skip 5s scan in pure Z-Score mode (pnl_tp>=50000 & pnl_sl<=-50000)
Step 2: Shared memory Pool initializer (avoid pickling df_main/df_5s per task)
Step 3: apply_overrides_fast without deepcopy (shallow copy for grid search)
Step 4: Pre-compute timestamps and hours arrays outside main loop
Step 5: Inline PnL calculation in 5s scan loop (avoid function call overhead)
Step 6: Add Calmar and Sortino ratios to compute_metrics + grid CSV
